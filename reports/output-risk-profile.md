# Output risks — Scaffold

Highest risks: making unsupported claims sound established; adding authorial experience; replacing the writer's position with the model's preferred one; imitating surface phrases without improving information order; inventing teaching-note quotations. Controls: claim-by-claim comparison, source/certainty preservation, conditional style moves, and checking exact excerpts against real inputs and outputs.

The v0.1.3 replay tests a longer draft plus a previously successful error-correction case. Mechanical checks cannot assess taste; model comparison cannot substitute for the user's preference. Published source prose is reference material, not a bank of sentences to paste into new work.
