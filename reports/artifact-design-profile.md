# Artifact design — editing plus brief teaching notes

Show the copyable English revision first, then 3–5 concise Vietnamese explanations anchored in actual before/after snippets. Include verification points only when substantive. Preserve the distinction between fictional test scenarios and real reporting. Do not force headings into a short article or make every paragraph end in a skeptical punchline.

For evaluation, show inputs and masked variants without labels revealing the condition; keep the answer key separate until a decision. Use plain Markdown files, not a dashboard or added interface. The overview is evaluation.md; there is no separate review viewer in this personal Scaffold.
