# Pilot 01 — sentence and paragraph editing

This is a synthetic draft created solely for testing. The province, survey and numbers below are fictional, not claims about Vietnam. Preserve that status. There are no external sources to research.

## User request

Edit this English paragraph for an international policy newsletter. Keep my position and all the qualifications, and explain the most useful changes in Vietnamese. You may reorganise it. Do not browse or add information.

## Draft

In this fictional example, the digitalisation of business registration procedures in Province A is an important development in relation to improving administrative efficiency. A survey of 120 small firms that used the new portal found that 72 of them still had to make an in-person visit. These are respondents to this survey, not all businesses in the province, and there was no survey before the portal opened. I think the portal is worth keeping, but attention should be paid to the fact that the existence of an online application channel does not necessarily mean that the whole process can be completed online. The survey did not ask firms how long a visit took. It is therefore not possible to say from this survey whether the portal has reduced the total time firms spend completing registration. The relevant question for the next round is which steps still require a visit and why.
