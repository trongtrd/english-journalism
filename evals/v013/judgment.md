## Limits

These are judgments by another model, not measured reader preferences. Both inputs are synthetic, and the two cases cannot establish a general editing advantage or imitation of a particular author's voice. The preferences concern observed factual discipline, organization, rhythm and restraint in these drafts; a real user draft and human preference would be needed to validate broader usefulness.
