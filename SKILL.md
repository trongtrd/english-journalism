# English Journalism

Turn the user's draft into an English newsletter draft for publication, aimed at an interested international reader who may lack Vietnam context. Edit structure as well as sentences when useful. Use the conditional editorial moves in [`references/style-guide.md`](references/style-guide.md), especially policy explained through ordinary consequences and pointed scrutiny of what evidence can actually establish.

## Workflow

1. Find the draft's central claim, its intended scope, and what the reader needs to understand first. Note any requested length, audience, title, and passages the writer wants kept. If the user explicitly wants only light correction, honor that limit.
2. Privately map each material assertion to its support in the draft: source or link, figure and denominator, date, speaker, certainty, and whether a scene is the writer's own observation. Keep allegations, forecasts, plans, estimates, and reported claims at their original level of certainty. Missing support stays missing.
3. Reorder when it helps: a supported everyday consequence can lead to the rule or institution behind it; a claimed success can lead to its measurement limit; a policy can move from mechanism to the people handling it. Choose the route the material earns. Do not force an anecdote, investigation, section headings, or rhetorical question into every piece.
4. Rewrite in fresh language. Attribute contested or sourced statements close to the claim. Explain Vietnamese institutions, acronyms, and units briefly using supplied facts. Keep the writer's viewpoint and degree of criticism; a sharper sentence must express an inference the draft already licenses. Use first person only for experiences or opinions the writer supplied. Preserve names, numbers, chronology, quotations, links, and the distinction between authorial prose and quoted speakers.
5. Compare the revision against the original claim by claim. If a crucial bridge or source is absent, flag it for the writer instead of inventing it. When the author's evidence notes qualify or correct a draft claim, use the provenance and certainty those notes support; do not carry an unconfirmed source label into the revision. If supplied notes demonstrate an error (such as arithmetic from given figures) or mark a quotation or experience as a placeholder, do not repeat it as fact. Correct a determinate error or omit/mark the unresolved placeholder, preserve the writer's separate position, and explain transparently what changed or was removed.

Before substantial structural editing, read the contrastive sets in [`references/editing-examples.md`](references/editing-examples.md) to choose whether reorganization earns its cost. For a requested light edit, honor that limit and do not load examples just to justify restructuring. The references are sufficient for ordinary use. No external tools or platform-specific APIs are required. When using an uploaded all-in-one guide, read the corresponding sections included in that document.

## Boundaries

Do not copy source phrasing, invent the writer's experiences, add a position the draft does not take, or silently turn a weak claim into a strong one. Do not browse or independently research unless asked. This skill edits supplied English; it does not translate, compose from scratch, or perform an independent fact-check by default.

## Quality Standard

Check that no vivid wording implies unsupported causation, exaggerates scale, converts a proposal into an enacted rule, or erases a caveat. The revised structure should make the policy or economic mechanism intelligible without losing the writer's point or attribution.

## Return

- A complete, copyable **English revision**, with a title only when appropriate to the input.
- **3–5 concise Vietnamese explanations** of meaningful edits. Each includes a short English excerpt from the original and its counterpart from the revision, followed by the reason in Vietnamese. Use actual text, not a paraphrase presented as a quotation. The after excerpt must show the stated change, including an added qualification when that is the lesson; an unchanged neighboring sentence is not a useful comparison. For an omission, show the original excerpt and label it removed. For a very short draft, give only the changes actually made.
- **Points for the writer to verify or decide**, only when there are real unresolved claims or choices. Do not silently supply missing reporting.

When the user asks for detailed analysis, expand the commentary. Otherwise keep the revised article foremost and the teaching notes brief.
